Small clips therefore carry a structural dust handicap that winner-scale
inventory amortizes much more easily.

## Mint repair counterexample

The 0.95 repair had two due episodes and one execution. In the 16:45 window:

- maker-sold five Up at 0.41;
- taker-sold five Down at 0.56, netting a 0.9528 pair and locking about -$0.236;
- the control maker-sold the same Down leg at 0.62 only 2.2 seconds later,
  completing at $1.03 for +$0.15.

The repair paid about $0.386 to remove two seconds of exposure. Across the
cohort it reduced neutral PnL from +$0.05 to -$0.34. Gen65 retains it as an A/B
control and adds a fee-inclusive $1.00 floor; no evidence supports lowering the
floor or crossing more aggressively.
