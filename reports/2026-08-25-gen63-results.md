Headline PnL does not rank the mechanics correctly. The T+270 basket arm made
slightly less realized PnL than baseline, but increased neutral economics by
$2.45 and improved the adverse floor by $4.95. In the 16:05 window it bought
five Up shares at 0.01 plus fee against five Down shares acquired at 0.96,
locking an approximately $0.9707 pair instead of retaining a $4.80 directional
bet. This is one successful repair, not a rate estimate.

The 180-second cutoff was not sufficient. It can stop fresh pairs but still
inherits an open leg created before its cutoff. Across the cohort its neutral
PnL was only +$0.49 and its worst case remained -$5.53.

The 20-set mint arm is rejected. Reopening inventory increased neutral spread,
but three incomplete five-share cycles converted +$4.35 neutral mechanics into
-$3.14 realized PnL. More inventory magnifies the known tail; it does not solve
completion.

The five-set mint repair encountered one due episode. It found only a 0.792
fee-inclusive pair sum and both depth and floor blocks, so it correctly did not
execute. It therefore remained identical to `mintcycle5` and did not reduce
risk. Lowering the 0.95 floor from one event would be unprincipled.

## Signal and latency

Gen63's four causal official-TWAP T+30 signs were +1.02, -0.36, -2.51, and
-1.65 bp; three matched the winner. The -2.51 bp window correctly identified
the first mint sale as toxic. Across Gen61-63, the sign is correct in six of
eight observations. The post-hoc `abs(signal) >= 2 bp` subset is 3/3, which is
too small and selected on the same data. It remains shadow-only.

The ordinary WebSocket event-age median was usually 9-13 ms. Local ordered-queue
residence stayed at or below 13 ms, while upstream/source-age tails reached
2.077 seconds. One second is not a realistic baseline action delay; it is an
intermittent feed-tail class that must freeze decisions and remain visible in
settlement results. Authenticated POST/cancel latency remains unmeasured.
